How Skillora Works

01

Discover a Skill

Browse courses, trending skills, and student mentors that align with your learning goals.

02

Connect with a Mentor

Book sessions, learn from student instructors, and participate in active campus and virtual learning communities.

03

Learn and Earn a Certificate

Complete a course, meet its attendance requirements, and request an authorized review. Certificates are Skillora learning records, not official college credentials unless separately authorized.
